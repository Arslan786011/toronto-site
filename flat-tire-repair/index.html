Can my flat tire be repaired?

Most punctures in the tread can be repaired safely. A tire usually can't be repaired when the damage is in the sidewall or shoulder, the hole is too large, or the tire was driven on flat. If it can't be fixed, we tell you straight and install your spare or a replacement tire.

Don't drive on a flat. Even a short distance can ruin the tire and bend the rim. Pull over somewhere safe and call us.
