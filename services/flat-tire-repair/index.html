What flat tire repair is

Most flat tires are caused by a nail, screw or other sharp object in the tread. Others come from a damaged valve stem or a slow leak where the tire meets the rim.

Some flats can be repaired and some cannot. A small puncture in the main tread is often repairable. A cut or bulge in the sidewall, or a tire that was driven on while flat for a long way, is usually not safe to repair and needs to be replaced. We look at the tire first and tell you your options before we do any work.

How our mobile service works

  1. Call (289) 212-8473 and tell us where you are and what happened.
  2. We give you a quick quote on the phone.
  3. We drive to you: your driveway, your work parking lot, or a safe spot at the side of the road.
  4. We check the tire and explain what we can do.
  5. You get back on the road without a trip to a shop.

We are open 24 hours a day, every day, so you can call at night and on weekends.

When to call us

  • Your tire is flat or looks low.
  • Your tire pressure light is on and the same tire keeps losing air.
  • You can see a nail or screw in the tread.
  • You hear a flapping or thumping sound while driving.
  • Your car pulls to one side.

Stay safe while you wait

If you are on a highway like the 401, the QEW or the DVP, pull as far off the road as you can, turn on your hazard lights and stay in a safe place. If you feel unsafe or there is danger, call 911 first.

Try not to keep driving on a flat tire. It can ruin the tire and damage the wheel, which turns a small repair into a bigger one.

What to have ready when you call

  • Where you are: an address, the nearest cross streets, or the highway and the nearest exit.
  • Your vehicle's make and model.
  • Which tire is flat.
  • The tire size, if you can see it. It is printed on the side of the tire, for example 225/65R17.
  • Your wheel lock key, if your car has locking wheel nuts.
  • Whether you have a spare tire.

FAQ

Questions about flat tire repair

Can you fix a flat tire where my car is parked?

Yes. Our service is mobile. We come to your home, your work or the roadside, anywhere in the GTA from Milton to Bowmanville.

Can every flat tire be repaired?

No. A puncture in the tread can often be repaired. Sidewall damage, large cuts, or a tire driven flat for a long way usually means the tire must be replaced. We will tell you before we start.

Are you open at night?

Yes. We are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

How much does mobile flat tire repair cost?

Call (289) 212-8473 for a quick quote. It depends on the tire, the damage and where you are.

What should I do if I get a flat on the highway?

Get as far off the road as you safely can, turn on your hazard lights and stay safe. Call 911 if there is danger. Then call us.

Can I drive on a tire with a slow leak?

It is not a good idea. A slow leak can quickly become a flat, and a low tire can overheat and fail. Call us to have it checked.
